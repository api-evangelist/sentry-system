Errors, traces, logs, and metrics overlap enough that it's hard to know which to use. Here's when to reach for each signal, with a real debugging walkthrough. The post explains that each signal answers a different question and feeds a different workflow, and that defaulting to what's familiar often means missing the value of other telemetry types.
